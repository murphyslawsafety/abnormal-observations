# ROX-0013 — Cross-Domain Measurement-Backaction Transfer

**Version:** 1.0
**Freeze date:** 2026-10-07
**ROH class:** ROH-BRIDGE
**Status:** FROZEN PROSPECTIVE LITERATURE-BRIDGE TEST
**Parent:** ROX-0012A BACKACTION_REGIME_CALIBRATION_PASS
**EUREKA status:** CANDIDATE ONLY
**Canonical EUREKA tally at freeze:** 6

## 1. Source architecture

ROX-0012A calibrated an established quantum architecture:

A physical measurement interaction changes subsequent system evolution, and the effect changes across measurement strength/frequency regimes.

Formal source signature:

S^- --I_lambda--> (Z,S^+) --T--> S_next

with:
- measurable dependence of later state/evolution on measurement strength or frequency;
- a lower-disturbance regime and a stronger-disturbance regime;
- no consciousness requirement.

## 2. Frozen cross-domain prediction

Before searching qualifying biological literature:

In live biological systems, a measurement procedure used to extract information will, at sufficiently high measurement dose/frequency, measurably change the biological state or dynamics being measured, while a lower-dose/lower-frequency or unmeasured control preserves more of the native dynamics.

The transferable object is measurement-as-transformation, not a shared microscopic mechanism.

## 3. Qualifying biological system

A system qualifies only if all are available:

1. a live biological preparation;
2. a measurement/readout procedure applied during ongoing dynamics;
3. at least two measurement exposures, frequencies, durations, or doses, OR one measurement condition plus a no/low-measurement control;
4. same biological preparation/background across compared conditions;
5. a measured downstream biological state or dynamic outcome;
6. evidence that increasing measurement burden changes that biological outcome;
7. enough source detail to distinguish measurement burden from unrelated treatment changes;
8. direct experimental data.

Preferred measurement families:
- fluorescence microscopy / live-cell imaging;
- calcium imaging;
- optical reporter readout;
- repeated biopsy/sampling only if the sampling itself plausibly perturbs the measured system and matched controls exist.

## 4. PASS

PASS requires >=2 independent biological systems satisfying all criteria.

At least one must show a dose/frequency dependence rather than only a single measured-vs-unmeasured contrast.

## 5. STRONG PASS

STRONG PASS requires:
- >=3 systems spanning >=2 materially different biological implementations;
- at least one explicit measurement-regime threshold/crossover or nonlinear boundary;
- at least one system with a matched sham/exposure control that helps separate physical measurement burden from unrelated experimental handling.

## 6. FAIL

FAIL if:
- fewer than 2 qualifying systems are found after targeted search; or
- apparent measurement effects are explainable only by unrelated treatment changes; or
- measurement merely increases noise without altering biological state/dynamics.

## 7. EUREKA-007 gate

A PASS does not automatically earn EUREKA-007.

EUREKA-007 may be declared only if:

A. this prediction was frozen before the qualifying biological evidence search;
B. >=2 independent systems qualify;
C. the transferred architecture is specifically measurement-as-transformation: stronger/more frequent readout changes the subsequent system state/dynamics;
D. at least one system includes a low/no-measurement comparator;
E. the claim is limited to cross-domain architecture, not universal observer physics;
F. no consciousness claim is made.

Potential title if earned:

Cross-domain measurement-backaction / observation-as-transformation transfer

## 8. Exclusions

Do not count:
- ordinary toxicity studies where the damaging exposure is not used as a measurement/readout;
- fixed/dead samples;
- retrospective data analysis;
- purely computational observer effects;
- studies where only image quality changes but biology does not;
- quantum systems, because they are the source calibration domain.

## 9. Claim boundary

Even a PASS would not establish:
- consciousness-caused collapse;
- that all observations perturb all systems;
- that Powerball outcomes are observer-dependent;
- a universal ROH law.

It would establish only a cross-domain architectural transfer: in both quantum and living systems, the physical act used to observe can become part of the dynamics under some regimes.
