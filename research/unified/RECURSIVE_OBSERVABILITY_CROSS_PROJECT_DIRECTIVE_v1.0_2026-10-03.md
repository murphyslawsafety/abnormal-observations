# RECURSIVE OBSERVABILITY CROSS-PROJECT DIRECTIVE v1.0

**Effective:** 2026-10-03  
**Scope:** LIFE-CODE, Reality-Code / Reality-Decompiler, ENERGY-CODE, Gravity-Code, Observer-Code, Unified-Code, Resonance-Code, Balance-Code, Powerball/physical-checksum experiments, and all explicit bridge experiments.  
**Status:** CROSS-PROJECT NORTH-STAR HYPOTHESIS / GOVERNANCE RULE — NOT AN EUREKA, NOT A PROVEN UNIVERSAL LAW

## 1. North-star hypothesis

All covered projects shall prospectively test, where scientifically meaningful, the following candidate architecture:

> **Recursive Observability Hypothesis (ROH):** Reality may admit a representation-independent architecture in which distinctions become records; records constrain later admissible transformations; observations are quotient/representation maps over a richer state; and sufficiently recursive systems can represent the world, their own observational relation to it, and eventually the rules by which that observation changes future state. Observer and observed are therefore treated as roles inside one coupled relational process rather than assumed to be fundamentally separate substances.

Compact progression:

`DISTINCTION -> RELATION -> TRANSFORMATION -> RECORD -> FEEDBACK -> MODEL -> SELF-MODEL -> RECURSIVE OBSERVATION`

Shorthand only:

`OBSERVATION OBSERVING ITSELF`

This shorthand is a research hypothesis, not a license to assume consciousness-dependent physics or that observation creates existence.

## 2. Formal working form

Let:
- `X` be a candidate substrate state space;
- `T` be an admissible transformation grammar;
- `q : X -> Z` be an observer/measurement/representation quotient;
- `r` be retained record state;
- `U` update records from observations;
- `M` be a model state when present.

Minimum observer architecture:

`z_t = q(x_t)`

`r_{t+1} = U(r_t, z_t)`

`x_{t+1} = T(x_t, r_t)`  (or a stochastic kernel with the same dependency structure)

Recursive-observer architecture, when scientifically justified:

`m_t = M(x_t, r_t, q, T)`

with future observation and/or action depending on `m_t`, so that the system can model its own observation/model relation.

No project may assume the recursive level merely because lower-level record/feedback structure is present.

## 3. Mandatory experiment rule

Every new substantive experiment in scope must explicitly state one of:

- `ROH-PROBE`: the experiment directly tests one or more ROH architectural components;
- `ROH-BRIDGE`: the experiment tests transfer of a frozen ROH-relevant structure across representations/domains;
- `ROH-CONTROL`: the experiment calibrates discrimination between ROH-relevant and decoy architectures;
- `ROH-NEUTRAL`: ROH is not relevant to the experiment and must not be imported into interpretation.

No result is permitted to become evidence for ROH merely because its language resembles observation, feedback, balance, hierarchy, recursion, or information.

## 4. Required architectural questions

Where applicable, experiments should distinguish:

1. Distinction / quotient: what states can the observer actually distinguish?
2. Record: what correlations persist and are available later?
3. Future dependence: do retained records constrain later transformations?
4. Grammar: is there a low-complexity transformation/composition law beneath the rendered observable?
5. Invariant closure: can invariants independently reconstruct the same admissible grammar?
6. Order / noncommutativity: does operation order contain information not reducible to unordered marginals?
7. Hierarchy: which structural level transfers—primitive, composition, module, or meta-grammar?
8. Identifiability: is the hidden realization unique, or only an equivalence class?
9. Observer recursion: does the system encode/model its own observation/model relation, rather than merely react to a record?
10. Nontrivial transfer: does source grammar reduce held-out target uncertainty beyond target-only calibration?

## 5. Grammar-first rule

For ROH-PROBE and ROH-BRIDGE experiments, do not begin by fitting rendered values when a lower-coordinate grammar can be tested first.

Preferred order:

`raw observations -> anonymous relations -> transformations/operators -> quotient/equivalence classes -> invariants/stabilizers -> composition grammar -> held-out transfer -> rendered checksum`

Implementation variables such as labels, machine identity, weight, weather, apparatus serial number, species name, physical units, or coordinate system are not substrate primitives unless the experiment independently earns that interpretation.

They may be used later to test whether a recovered abstract grammar is implementation-specific or implementation-invariant.

## 6. Dual-reconstruction rule

A hidden grammar is not accepted from trajectory/prediction fit alone.

Infer independently:
- operation/transformation algebra from interventions/compositions;
- invariant/stabilizer/closure structure from preserved relations.

Require mutual reconstruction within frozen tolerances.

High fit with failed closure is not ROH evidence.

## 7. Recursive-level evidence rule

Evidence for ordinary observation/record formation is not automatically evidence for self-observation.

To support the specifically recursive layer, a result must demonstrate at least one of:
- a model/record of the observer's own observational map affecting later behavior;
- second-order model comparison (model of model / observation of observation) with prospective consequence;
- a frozen self-referential update law that improves held-out prediction beyond a non-reflexive capacity-matched control;
- an equivalent formally rigorous result with explicit assumptions.

## 8. Cross-project firewall

All project tracks remain scientifically distinct.

A result in LIFE-CODE, Reality-Code, ENERGY-CODE, Gravity-Code, Observer-Code, Unified-Code, Resonance-Code, Balance-Code, or a lottery experiment may motivate a bridge but cannot validate another track without a dedicated frozen bridge experiment.

Analogy is not transfer.
Shared vocabulary is not transfer.
Shared equations are not transfer unless the mapping itself is prospectively constrained and nontrivial.

## 9. Proof ladder

The universal ROH claim is not considered established by accumulation of suggestive similarities.

The evidence ladder is:

**R0 — Formal coherence**  
The architecture is mathematically well-defined and non-tautological.

**R1 — Controlled discrimination**  
A frozen pipeline distinguishes recursive-observability architectures from matched decoys and pseudo-invariants.

**R2 — Natural single-domain recovery**  
A real natural/physical system supports the architecture prospectively under held-out tests.

**R3 — Independent domain replication**  
The same abstract signature succeeds in a second unrelated natural domain without semantic refitting.

**R4 — Nontrivial cross-domain transfer**  
Source structure reduces target held-out uncertainty beyond capacity-matched target-only calibration.

**R5 — Recursive observer test**  
A real system demonstrates prospective second-order observer/model feedback not explained by a non-reflexive matched control.

**R6 — Universal-candidate review**  
Only after multiple unrelated natural-domain replications, hostile nulls, identifiability audits, prior-art review, and reconciliation may a universal ROH claim be considered.

No single lottery win, biological pattern, physics fit, synthetic benchmark, philosophical argument, or observer experiment can skip this ladder.

## 10. Falsification rules

ROH must remain falsifiable.

Potential falsifiers include:
- no low-complexity grammar surviving held-out transfer;
- target-only models fully explaining apparent source transfer;
- operation-derived and invariant-derived structures disagreeing;
- order effects disappearing under controls;
- observer-equivalent states remaining indistinguishable under every authorized prospective probe;
- recursive/self-model terms adding no held-out information over matched non-reflexive controls;
- apparent cross-domain structure collapsing under representation or semantic relabeling;
- claimed universal signatures failing independent natural-domain replication.

A FAIL remains a first-class result and may not be reinterpreted as support for a deeper hidden ROH layer without a new experiment ID and new discriminating evidence.

## 11. Evidence classes and language

Every ROH-related result must label evidence as:
- empirical;
- formal/mathematical;
- architectural/methodological;
- ontological interpretation.

Do not borrow authority across classes.

Until R6 is legitimately reached, preferred language is:
- "supports the candidate architecture";
- "consistent with recursive observability";
- "transfers the frozen abstract signature";
- "does not support / falsifies the tested representation".

Do not state as established fact that observation is required for existence, that consciousness creates reality, or that the universe has purpose.
